# Product Requirements

CareBridge helps a person move from an emergency report to a clear next action without pretending to be a medical professional. The core experience is deliberately short: one emergency CTA, voice or text input, structured reported concerns, deterministic urgency, trusted facility and clinician matching, and explicit actions.

The MVP must support English, Hindi, and common Hinglish. It must continue working when voice, location, an external AI service, live information, or the database is unavailable. The result must answer four questions: what to do, where to go, who can help, and how to get there.

The product must never diagnose or recommend treatment. It must not invent a hospital, doctor, phone number, live status, or successful call. Critical actions remain user-triggered through the phone dialer, Google Maps deep link, or native share sheet.

Acceptance is a complete mobile demo of the supplied Hinglish scenario, including a visible emergency classification, trusted facility, linked clinician resource, matching explanation, 112 action, navigation, share/copy fallback, and handoff summary.
